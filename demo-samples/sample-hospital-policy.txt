Riverside Community Clinic — Patient Admission and Billing Policy

Admission Process
The admission process requires the patient to complete registration before the medical evaluation. Patients must bring a valid photo ID and current insurance card to their appointment. Walk-in admissions are accepted between 8 AM and 6 PM on weekdays. Same-day appointments are subject to provider availability and are not guaranteed.

Insurance and Billing
All billing disputes must be submitted in writing within 60 days of the statement date. Insurance claims are filed automatically on behalf of the patient once a visit is completed. Patients are responsible for any applicable copay at the time of visit. Uninsured patients may qualify for a sliding-scale discount based on household income; documentation is required at intake.

Refund Policy
Patients may request a refund of an overpayment within 30 days of the billing statement if the amount was paid in error. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after approval by the billing department. To request a refund, contact patient billing support with the statement number and payment confirmation.

Prescription Policy
Prescription refills require a minimum of 48 hours notice. Controlled substance refills require an in-person visit and cannot be authorized over the phone. Patients transferring prescriptions from another provider must submit a signed transfer request.

Cancellation Policy
Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice may be subject to a missed-appointment fee. Repeated no-shows may result in a requirement to prepay for future visits.

Data Privacy
Riverside Community Clinic does not use any generative AI model to process patient records. All internal document search tools rely on traditional information retrieval techniques such as TF-IDF and BM25 ranking to help staff locate policy information quickly, ensuring the system never fabricates information that is not present in the source policy documents.
